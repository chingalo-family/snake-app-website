Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 August 2026 · Applies to Snake App by Chingalo Family

Snake App (“the App”) is published by Chingalo Family. This policy explains what information the App handles and how we treat it. We designed Snake App to be offline-first: you can play without creating an account on our servers.

1. Who we are

Chingalo Family develops and publishes Snake App for mobile and desktop platforms. Contact us using the details on our website contact section, or:

  • Phone: +255 687 168 627
  • WhatsApp: +255 742 349 206
  • Instagram: @chingalo_family

2. Information the App stores

Depending on how you use Snake App, the following may be stored on your device:

  • Local player profile (optional): username, full name, and optionally email and phone number you enter.
  • Game progress: high scores, best combos, games played, and highest level unlocked.
  • Settings: preferences such as sound effects, background music, haptics, control hints, and language.
  • App metadata: version information used to show About and to open the store listing when you check for updates.

Creating a profile is only required if you want to save high scores and level progress. You may play without a profile; in that case those results are not persisted.

3. What we do not do (for core play)

  • We do not require a cloud account or password for core gameplay.
  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • Optional email and phone on the profile are for your local profile display and future features you choose to use; they are stored on-device unless a future version clearly offers sync and you opt in.

4. Updates and store links

On Android and iOS, “Check for updates” may open the Google Play Store or Apple App Store listing for Snake App. Those stores process data under their own privacy policies when you use them.

5. Analytics, ads, and children

The current Snake App experience is focused on local play. If we add advertising or analytics in a future release, we will update this policy and, where required, request appropriate consent.

Snake App is a general-audience game. If you allow a child to use the App, please supervise profile fields (especially optional email or phone) and device settings.

6. Data retention and deletion

Local profile and progress remain on your device until you clear the App’s data, uninstall the App, or use any in-app controls we provide in the future to reset progress. Uninstalling removes locally stored App data according to your operating system.

7. Your choices

  • Play without creating a profile.
  • Leave optional email and phone blank.
  • Turn off sound effects, music, or haptics in Settings.
  • Contact us to ask questions about this policy.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App changes. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the App after an update means you accept the revised policy.

9. Contact

For privacy questions about Snake App, contact Chingalo Family via +255 687 168 627, WhatsApp, or Instagram.
